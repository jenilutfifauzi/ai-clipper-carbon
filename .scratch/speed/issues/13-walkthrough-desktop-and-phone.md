# 13: Walkthrough, desktop and phone

**Parent spec:** `spec.md` in this feature folder (all user stories); ADR 0009.

**What to build:** a hands-on pass of the whole feature in the real app, on a desktop browser and on an iPhone, with what is found written up here and fixed or ticketed.

- Speed on Source and media Segments and on a video Overlay, at 0.25×, 0.5×, 2×, 4×, 8× and 16×, with and without the pitch switch; captions and Word Edits on a sped Source Segment.
- Reverse on a Source Segment with a Split Layout, a media Segment and an Overlay; the wait for a copy; split and trim of a reversed Segment.
- Freeze frames of the Source and of a video, their length dragged, a Transition into one, a slow zoom on one.
- The rendered Clip against the preview for each.
- On the iPhone: memory with a reversed Source Segment (its extra videos), the Speed sheet, Freeze frame from the toolbar.
- The agent: retiming a Clip and making a Freeze frame through MCP alone.

**Blocked by:** 01–12.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Each finding fixed, or ticketed with a reason.
