# 09: The preview beyond 0.25–4×, and the sweep of unused copies

**Parent spec:** `spec.md` in this feature folder (user story 14; Solution "The preview"; Implementation Decisions "Retimed copies"); ADR 0009.

**What to build:** the preview plays any Speed with its sound, as the render will, and Retimed copies nobody uses any more are removed.

- **Preview copies.** A Segment or a video Overlay at a Speed outside 0.25–4× plays a preview-size Retimed copy of its range, retimed at its Speed (and reversed if it is), with its sound made by the render's chain. It is played at the preview rate. The copy is named by its Speed and pitch too. While it is being made, the held first frame and progress show, as in 06.
- **The sweep.** When a Job is opened, Retimed copies of its Source, and of media items only its Candidates use, that no Candidate nor any state in their Revision History uses are removed. Copies still being made are left.

**Blocked by:** 03, 04, 06.

**Status:** done

- [x] A Segment at 8× plays in the preview with its sound (pitch kept), in an eighth of its range, and leaves at its Timeline end.
- [x] A Segment at 0.125× plays in the preview with its sound, at eight times its range.
- [x] Changing a Segment from 8× to 2× plays it on the decks again with no copy.
- [x] The sweep keeps a copy used only by an older Revision in the Revision History and removes one used by none.
- [x] The sweep never removes a copy that is being written.

## Comments

**2026-10-17: done for media Segments and video Overlays (and their sound). Source Segments beyond 0.25–4× on the decks wait for 07 (see below), so the third box is met only for media Segments.**

**What was built:**
- **Copies at a Speed have the right frames.** `_reverse_in_pieces` now brings a retimed copy's picture back to its item's frame rate (`Copies.frame`'s fps, the Source's for 07's Job copies): `settb=AVTB,setpts=PTS/speed−0.001 frame,fps=…:round=up`. Each frame of the copy is the last of the item's at or before its time (8×: frames 0, 8, 16…; 0.125×: each frame 8 times). Each piece's sound (the render's `speed_filters`) is padded or cut to the piece's length (`apad`/`atrim`), because `atempo` drops some of a short input's end: at 0.125× a 0.5 s piece came out 0.15 s short, and the joins drifted. Tested on single-piece and multi-piece copies (4×, 0.125×, 8× reversed, 0.25×, 6× reversed): frame by frame, video length within half a frame, sound within 50 ms, and the pitch kept at 0.125×.
- **One rule for "which copy does this item play from?"**, twinned:
  - backend `retimed.copies_played(start, end, speed, reverse, pitch)` → `[(CopySize, Retime)]`: in Reverse, the full-size reversed copy for the render; for the preview, a copy retimed (and reversed if it is) outside `PLAYABLE_SPEEDS` (0.25–4), else in Reverse the 1× reversed one. `copies_used(candidates, video durations)` applies it to every Segment (not Freeze frames) and video Overlay (range `offset .. offset + secs × speed`, cut at the video's end), keyed by media id or `None` for the Source.
  - frontend `retimedCopy.ts`: `previewCopyOf({start, end, speed, reverse, pitch})` → `RetimedRange | null`; `segmentCopy(segmentPlan)` (any Segment of the plan, the Source's too: meant for 07's decks) and `eventCopy(overlayOrSoundEvent)`. `mediaPlay(segment, end, copy)` and `eventPlay(event, file, copy)` give `{src, span}` for `follow`. A reversed-only copy plays at the item's Speed; a retimed one at the preview rate only (`follow`'s rate = preview rate × 1), with `preservesPitch` on. Until it is made, the item's file is held at the copy's first frame (in Reverse the last frame before the range's end, forwards its start), and the Timeline time runs on.
  - `useRetimedCopies(asks)` (several at once, for `PreviewMusic`) and `useRetimedCopy(media, range)`; made copies are cached by media, range, direction, Speed and pitch. `copyProgress(range, copy)`: "Reversing… n%" / "Retiming… n%", or "Couldn't reverse/retime this video". `RetimedRange` in `api.ts` gained `speed` and `pitch`; `PLAYABLE_SPEEDS` is exported from `follow.ts`.
- **Where it's used:** `MediaSegment` (PreviewSegments.tsx) shows its progress as before. `PreviewOverlays` plays the Overlay's copy (muted) with the progress at the top of the frame while it shows. `PreviewMusic` plays an Overlay's sound from the same copy (same range and pitch, so one copy serves both), silent until it is made.
- **The sweep:** `GET /api/jobs/{id}` (what the Job page and the Advanced Edit load, once on opening) queues `api.sweep_retimed` as a `BackgroundTask`, which runs after the answer is sent. It collects the Job's Candidates and every state in their Revision History (`store.revision_history`) and uses `copies_used` to work out what they play from. Then `retimed.sweep(folder, used)` removes every copy in `jobs/<id>/retimed/` that serves none of them (`Retime.covers`, so a wider copy that serves a Segment is kept), and also in `media/<id>.retimed/` for each video the Job's states use whose `media_uses` (the delete check) lists no other Job's Candidate. It also removes the hidden `.<name>.<uuid>.part.mp4` and `.<name>.<uuid>.pieces` leftovers, and leaves any other file alone. **Copies being written** are registered in a new `retimed._writing` set (target, part, pieces folder) for the whole of `_reverse_in_pieces`, by the editor's asks and by renders alike (`_asked` alone would miss a render's), and the sweep holds `_writing_lock` while it runs.
- Tests: `backend/tests/test_preview_copies.py` (the rule; the sweep through the API: kept by an older Revision, kept when a wider copy serves, removed when used by none, a pitch variant or another Speed removed, leftovers removed, the Source's folder swept, a video another Job uses kept by both; a real copy stopped mid-write while the sweep runs; non-copy files left). `test_retimed_copies.py`'s Speed test is now frame by frame. `retimedCopy.test.ts`: the rule, 8× (rate 1, pitch kept, an eighth of its range, leaves at its end), the preview rate on a copy, 0.125×, the held first frame while it's made, 8× → 2× back on its own file, an Overlay at 16× and at 2×.

**What remains for Source Segments (07, or whoever lands after it):**
- The decks (`playback.ts`) still play a Source Segment outside 0.25–4× at `previewSpeed`'s clamp, moved back in step as before. Once 07's per-Segment copy playback is in (the Segment's own master and follower, parked like a media Segment's video), send to that path every Source Segment for which `segmentCopy(segment)` is non-null, not only the reversed ones. Play it as `mediaPlay`'s `fromCopy` does: from `copy.at` at the preview rate when the copy is retimed, at the Segment's Speed when it is only reversed. Until it's made, hold its first frame.
- `useRetimedCopies`/`RetimedAsk` name a Media Library id and call `api.retimedCopy` (`/api/media/{id}/retimed`). Source copies need the ask to name the Job (e.g. `{ job, range }`, its key and 07's `POST /api/jobs/{id}/retimed`) with the same `{start, end, reverse, speed, pitch}` body. The backend `RetimedRequest` already takes all five.
- The sweep already counts Source Segments: `copies_used` keys them under `None`, and `sweep_retimed` sweeps `jobs/<id>/retimed/`. So 07's copies need nothing more as long as they're named by `Retime.name`.

**Decided differently / worth knowing:**
- **The render's frames at 4× and 8× are one later than the copy's.** The renderer's `setpts=(PTS-STARTPTS)/speed,fps=<rate>` with `fps`'s default rounding puts a frame exactly between two output frames into the later one, so at 4× it shows frames 1, 5, 9… where a copy shows 0, 4, 8… (2× and 0.5× agree). That's one frame of the item at extreme speed. I left the renderer alone (shared file); a later ticket could give it the copy's `round=up` chain.
- **What the sweep doesn't consult:** other Jobs' Revision Histories. A video that another Job's *current* Candidates use is left alone entirely, by either Job, so its unused copies stay until the item is deleted. An item used only in another Job's history may lose copies, which are made again when asked. The sweep is per Job, as the spec says. The MCP's `get_job` doesn't sweep: it isn't the editor opening the Job.
- Overlays have no `reverse` yet (the glossary mentions an Overlay in Reverse, but no ticket models it): `copies_used` and `eventCopy` treat Overlays as forwards.
- The media Segment's sound plays from the copy's own sound (the render's chain) at the preview rate; its volume and volume Keyframes apply as before.

**2026-10-17 (07): the Source Segments' part is in, so the third box is met for them too.** `onOwnVideos` in `playback.ts` sends every Source Segment with a preview copy (reversed, or outside 0.25–4×: the same rule as `segmentCopy`) to its own master and follower (`CopiedSourceSegment` in `PreviewSegments.tsx`, played by `sourcePlay`, which is `fromCopy` with the Source's proxy and frame); the decks wait at the next Segment they play. `RetimedAsk` is `{ media, range }` or `{ job, range }` (`api.sourceRetimedCopy`, `POST /api/jobs/{id}/retimed`). `retimedCopy.test.ts`: a Source Segment at 8× plays its retimed copy at the preview rate on its own videos; changed to 2×, it has no copy and plays on the parked deck at 2×. 03's playback test "a Source Segment past 4× plays at 4× on its deck until it has a copy" now checks that it runs on the clock off the decks.

**Review fixes (2026-10-17): copies are asked for once settled.** The Speed slider dragged past 4× changed the plan at every step, and `useRetimedCopies` asked for a copy at each, filling the backend's queue with stale asks. Its effect is now `askCopies(asks, heard)` (exported, free of React): each copy not known yet is asked for only after it has stayed wanted `SETTLE_MS` (300 ms); a new set of asks stops the one before it, so a step dragged past is never sent. Polling is as before, and an answer that arrives after the asks changed still caches a made copy but no longer schedules a poll. The inspector and the preview show "Retiming… 0%" during those 300 ms. `retimedCopy.test.ts` "asking for copies" (fake timers): 5×…8× in 50 ms steps asks once, at 8×, polls until made, and a made copy isn't asked for again.

**Review fixes (2026-10-17): the render shows a copy's frames at any Speed.** A sped Segment's picture in `renderer._segmented` is now `settb=AVTB,setpts=(PTS-STARTPTS)/speed − 0.001 frame,fps=<rate>:round=up`, the Retimed copies' chain, so a frame landing between two of the Clip's goes to the later one in both: at 4× the render shows frames 0, 4, 8… as the preview copy does (it showed 1, 5, 9…). `test_retimed_copies.py::test_at_4x_the_render_shows_the_same_frames_as_the_preview_copy` renders a 4× media Segment and compares it frame by frame with a 4× preview copy. `scripts/check_ffmpeg.py` now requires every filter the renderer, its frame processes, the Retimed copies and the Scene analysis use (among them `tpad`, `reverse`, `areverse`, `apad`, `atrim`, `settb`, `xfade`, `acrossfade`, `loudnorm`); all pass with ffmpeg-full.
