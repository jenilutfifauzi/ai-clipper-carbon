# 02: Cut out a Source Segment in the render (tracer)

**Parent spec:** `../spec.md` ("Model", "Matte store and runner", "Planner", "Renderer", "Editor", "MCP"); ADR 0015.

**What to build:** the owner switches on "Remove background" for a Source Segment (or several picked at once), in any Layout. The Inspector shows "Finding the subject… N%" while the Matte is made in the background. The rendered Clip shows only the speaker, their surroundings replaced by their own picture blurred (the default Background). In this ticket the preview still shows the picture whole; 03 draws the cut. The agent can do the same through MCP and follow the Matte's progress.

**The model:** a Segment gains an optional `background_removal` = `{subject, feather}`. `subject` is `person` only, and `feather` is 0–40 output px at the picture's fitted size (default 4). An unknown subject or a value out of range is refused on every save path, naming the item. An absent field is off.

**The Matte:**
- A Matte module keeps pieces of 10 s aligned to the Source's timeline, keyed by subject, model version and piece start. Each piece is a grayscale video at a 1280 px long edge, at the Source's frame rate, one Matte frame per frame.
- Its interface, for a range, says which pieces exist, are missing, in progress or failed. It enqueues the missing ones, gives progress, and retries.
- The model sits behind a narrow matter seam: frames in at model size, alpha frames out, with recurrent state kept across a run. The real matter runs RVM through onnxruntime with CoreML ahead of the CPU. Each piece starts 1 s early to warm up, and the warm-up frames are dropped.
- A runner (like the Retimed copies') makes pieces one at a time, outside the Worker, at low priority, latest request first. Saves that switch Background removal on or change a range ask for them.
- A failure is remembered per piece until a Retry.

**The render:**
- The planner puts Background removal (feather scaled by the item's scale) and the pieces read on the SegmentPlan, and the dry-run view shows it.
- The renderer reads the Matte with the same `-ss`/`-t` as the picture, follows the Layout's crop and scale, feathers it, and `alphamerge`s it after crop, scale and flips, before the Grade and Video Effects.
- It composites the result over the Segment's picture blurred (the default Background, used in every Layout; in Blur it replaces the Layout's fill). All of this happens before the joins.
- A render makes the missing pieces first, inside its job, with progress. A failed piece, or the model absent, fails the render with a message naming the item and the cause.

## Comments

**Built:**
- Model: `BackgroundRemoval {subject: Subject.PERSON, feather}` on `Segment.background_removal` (backend/app/candidates.py; `FEATHER_MAX` 40, default 4). The feather is checked by the item (`checked_background_removal`), so the refusal names it ("Segment a's background_removal feather is 41; it is 0 to 40"); an unknown subject is pydantic's enum refusal. A lone cut-out Segment of the Source as filmed stays a Segment (review.py). Frontend: `BackgroundRemoval`, `FEATHER_MAX`/`FEATHER_DEFAULT` in api.ts.
- Matte module `backend/app/matte.py`:
  - `Matter` (the seam: `version` class attribute, `__init__(model_path)`, `run()` → a callable frame RGB H×W×3 uint8 → alpha H×W uint8, recurrent state per run). `RvmMatter` (version `rvm-mnv3-1`): onnxruntime, CoreML ahead of CPU (CPU alone if the session fails), `downsample_ratio = min(1, 480 / long edge)` (0.375 at 1280). Checked by hand on the real file: CoreML takes 282 of 303 nodes; about 0.2 s a frame at 1280×720 on CoreML and CPU alike (for 09 to measure properly).
  - `Mattes(folder, video, (w, h, fps), duration)`: one item's pieces. `matte_pieces(start, end, duration)` gives the 10 s piece starts a range reads. Files: `jobs/<id>/mattes/{subject}-{version}-{start}.mp4`, failure `{subject}-{version}-{start}.failed` (its message) beside it until `retry`. A piece is H.264 **yuv420p** (grayscale content, so a browser plays it for 03; ffmpeg 9 maps gray↔tv-range yuv exactly), keyframe each second, `+faststart`, at most 1280 px on the long edge (a smaller Source at its own size: ADR 0015 amended by those words), the Source's fps, exactly `round(end·fps) − round(start·fps)` frames (the last frame held if the decode comes short). Runs from 1 s before the piece (from 0 for the first), warm-up frames dropped. Decode and encode are ffmpeg pipes; written to `.part` and renamed.
  - `MatteRunner(person_model, matter_type, ffmpeg)`: app-wide (`app.state.mattes`, shared with `Renders.mattes`; the CLI makes its own). `ask(wanted)` queues missing pieces as the latest request (a request's pieces in order) on one daemon thread, ffmpegs `nice -n 10`; nothing is queued without the model (status "waiting"). `status(wanted)` → `MatteStatus(state ready|making|waiting|failed|missing, progress, error)`. `retry(wanted)`. `make(wanted, progress)` is the render's synchronous path (raises `ModelAbsent` / the remembered failure). One piece at a time across runner and render (`_one` lock). The model (onnxruntime) runs in-process, so only its ffmpegs are at low priority.

**Deviations / gotchas:**
- ffmpeg 9's concat demuxer with an input `-ss` gives wrong timestamps (frames from 1 s, or none): the render reads the listing from its start (`-t offset+span+1`) and trims in the graph (`trim=start=…,setpts=PTS-offset/TB`), keeping the picture's timestamps exactly. Offsets use `round(piece·fps)/fps`.
- Speed and Freeze frame get the same timing filters on the Matte as on the picture (so they likely already work forwards), but are untested; Reverse and keyed Manual Scenes are refused at render (07).
- The feather of a Source Segment in a Manual Layout Override (not the Candidate's Layout) isn't scaled by that override's scale (1).

**For 03 (preview compositor):** the plan's SegmentPlan has `background_removal.feather` (as drawn, 1080×1920 units; it is gblur's sigma, i.e. CSS `blur(<feather>px)`), `background`, and `matte: number[]` (piece starts, 10 s each, on the Source's timeline). Pieces are served from the Job's directory: `/media/<job>/mattes/person-<version>-<start>.mp4` (version `rvm-mnv3-1`, or the test matter's; the status endpoint doesn't give it, so expose `MatteRunner.version` via the plan or the endpoint if needed). A piece's frame k is the Source's frame `round(start·fps) + k`. The Matte is drawn through the same Scenes as the picture (`_matte_scene`). The status endpoint tells which pieces are ready only per Segment (all or nothing); 03 may want a per-piece list.
**For 04 (Background colour/image):** `_backdrop_scene(..., background=segment.background)` already draws `BackgroundKind.COLOUR`; add `image` there and set `SegmentPlan.background` from the Segment's own field in `planner._cut_out`.
**For 05 (Behind Overlays):** lay them in `_cut_out` between `[{name}b]` (backdrop) and `[{name}c]` (cut picture), at Timeline time (the Segment's chain is in its own time from 0: offset by `segment.start`).
**For 06:** media Segments/Overlays: `Mattes` per media item (folder beside the item), `planner._cut_out` currently returns {} for `media`; `rendering.matted` filters `media is None`; the UI's `cuttableSegments` filters `!segment.media`.
**For 08 (sweep):** pieces are `mattes/{subject}-{version}-{start}.mp4` plus `.failed` files and `.*.part.mp4`; `matte_wanted` gives what a Candidate uses. Remove-model refusal: check the runner is idle (`MatteRunner._making`/`_requests`; add a public `idle()`).
**For 09 (real model):** `RvmMatter(model_path).run()`; drive it through `Mattes.make(ffmpeg, matter, Subject.PERSON, piece)` for throughput.
