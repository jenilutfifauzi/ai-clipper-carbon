# Glossary

Use these terms in code, UI copy, tickets and commit messages.

## Jobs and selection

- **Job:** one episode being processed, from its URL, upload or a file on this Mac to its Clips.
- **Source:** the episode video. Times "on the Source's timeline" are seconds into it.
- **Transcript / Words:** the episode's normalized text with per-Word timing.
- **Window:** a Transcript slice scored by the AI.
- **Candidate:** a proposed clip under review: a range of the Source (or, once cut in the Advanced Edit, its Segments), its hook text, Layout, style and edits.
- **Clip:** a rendered output of a Candidate: one per Candidate, replaced by each render, which records the Export Settings it was made with.
- **Channel Profile / Brief:** standing and per-Job steering for Selection.
- **Reference Set:** past clips used for evaluation and few-shot examples.

## Rendering

- **Layout:** how a Scene is framed: Track, Split, Blur or Manual (Auto: chosen per Scene by its faces, never Manual).
- **Manual:** the Layout that frames the Source by hand with a Framing, still for the whole Scene: face tracking is not run there. The Candidate's Layout may be Manual (one Framing for every Scene without an override), and so may a Layout Override (its own Framing). See docs/adr/0007-one-transform-for-every-picture-and-a-manual-layout.md.
- **Transform:** where and how a picture sits on the frame: its centre in output pixels (kept on the frame), its scale over its fitted size (the largest that fits 1080×1920; 1 fits), its stretch across and down, its rotation (degrees clockwise), its flips and its opacity. Constant over its item's length, except where its position, scale, rotation or opacity has Keyframes.
- **Crop:** what is cut off each edge of a picture before it is fitted, as fractions of its width and height (at most 0.9 each, a tenth always kept).
- **Framing:** a Transform, a Crop and a Background: how a media Segment, or the Source in a Manual Scene, fills the frame.
- **Background:** what shows behind a Framing's picture where it doesn't cover the frame: the picture itself, blurred, or a colour.
- **Scene:** a shot between two cuts.
- **Render Plan:** the planner's output for one Clip (Scenes with their panels, caption events, and the hook event). It is everything the renderer draws.
- **Export Settings:** how a Clip is encoded: its codec (H.264, HEVC or AV1), resolution (720p to 4K), frame rate (the Source's, or one chosen), quality (High, Recommended or Smallest, or a bitrate of its own) and loudness target (or none). The Render Plan stays in 1080×1920 output pixels whatever the resolution, and the render draws it at that size; only the frame rate changes the plan. Changing them never makes a Clip stale.
- **Export Preset:** a named, global set of Export Settings that a render uses for its whole batch (the default Preset when none is chosen). A Clip remembers its Preset, and rendering it again uses that Preset as it is now (the default once it is deleted). Built-in Presets can't be changed or deleted; the owner saves, renames and deletes their own, and picks the default. Not a Style Preset. See docs/adr/0021-export-presets-and-a-plan-drawn-at-any-size.md.
- **Export file:** a frame (PNG) or the sound alone (WAV or M4A) saved from a Candidate on request, kept in its Job. Not a Clip: it is never stale and never in the render queue.
- **Hook:** the opening overlay: the Candidate's hook text (with any Emphasis) and its Kicker, drawn as its Hook Style says.

## Clip editor

- **Style Preset:** a named, global look for captions, the hook, the Watermark and joins: a Caption Style, a Hook Style, a Watermark Style and a Join Transition. Built-in Presets can't be changed or deleted.
- **Caption Style:** how captions look and are broken into Cues and lines: font, size, colours, outline, drop shadow (as a Text Layer's), tracking, case, highlight (and the highlight box's padding and corners), position (one of four, or wherever the captions are dragged on the preview: every Cue without its own goes there), and line limits (lines per Cue, characters and Words per line, longest Cue, the pause that ends one). Phrase and Sentence fill the line limits in one go (one short line; up to two lines, to a sentence's end); no mode is kept.
- **Cue:** one block of captions shown at once, of one to three lines, from the Words it holds. What an SRT or VTT cue is.
- **Cue Edit:** a change to one Cue, keyed, as a Word Edit is, by the time of its first Word: the Cue starts at that Word, moved in time with its highlight or shown earlier or later at either end (in seconds of the Source's or the item's timeline), and at its own place and a size scaled from the Caption Style's; or, instead, that Word's Cue joined to the one before where only a pause, a sentence's end or the longest Cue would part them. It does nothing while that Word isn't shown, and is kept. It belongs to its Candidate.
- **Hook Style:** how the hook looks and moves: Shape, font, size, case, lines, alignment, line height, tracking, colours, outline, drop shadow (as a Text Layer's), its box's padding and corners, Quote Mark, Kicker colours, position (one of three, or wherever it is dragged on the preview), and animations.
- **Hook Shape:** how the hook's box is drawn: one block behind all its lines, a strip per line, or none.
- **Emphasis:** words of the hook text marked `*like this*`, drawn in the Hook Style's Emphasis colour.
- **Kicker:** an optional short label (at most 24 characters) in a pill above the hook, such as "TERBONGKAR!".
- **Quote Mark:** a badge with quote marks on the hook's top-left corner.
- **Watermark:** a line of text or a Logo shown over the whole Clip, under the captions and the hook, at one of nine places in the safe area or wherever it is dragged on the preview.
- **Watermark Style:** what the Watermark is (off, text or a Logo), its text's font, colour and outline, its place, size and opacity.
- **Logo:** an image the owner uploads for Watermarks, kept as a PNG for every Job. Deleting one leaves the Presets and Candidates that showed it with no Watermark.
- **Style Override:** a field of the Preset's style replaced on one Candidate.
- **Join Transition:** the Transition (or none) that a Style Preset gives every new join of its Candidates: any two Segments that come to meet with no Transition between them, whether by a split, an insert, a paste, a Freeze frame, a delete or a reorder, in the editor or by the agent. It is copied, never linked: changing the Preset, or switching to another, leaves existing joins as they are.
- **Word Edit:** a correction of one caption Word, keyed by a time on the Source's timeline, or on a media item's for its Media Words. It gives new text, removes the Word (empty text), or starts a new line before it (and a new Cue when its Cue has all its lines). It belongs to its Candidate.
- **Layout Override:** a Layout for the one Scene that holds a time on the Source's timeline, replacing the Candidate's Layout there. A Manual one carries its Framing.
- **Preview Plan:** the Render Plan of a Candidate, or of an unsaved draft of its edits, computed for the editor's preview without rendering.
- **Revision:** one saved state of a Candidate, numbered from 1; each saved change makes the next. An edit made from an older Revision is refused, so that no editor (the clip editor, the Advanced Edit, another tab or the agent) saves over another's change without knowing. The editor then merges its unsaved changes onto the newer Revision, item by item; where both changed the same item, the owner's change in front of them wins and they are told. Choosing a Candidate for rendering (selected) is not a change of its Revision.
- **Revision History:** the Candidate's last 50 saved states kept on this Mac, one per stretch of saving from one editor (a new one when another editor saves, or after a pause), always including the state just before another editor's change. Restoring one saves it again as the next Revision; nothing is lost.
- **Timeline:** the editor's tracks under the preview: the clip's range, Scenes, Cues and the hook. The Advanced Edit has its own Timeline of Tracks, in Timeline time.

## Advanced Edit

- **Advanced Edit:** a CapCut-like workspace for one Candidate, opened from the clip editor: assets, player, inspector and a multi-track Timeline (on a phone: player, Timeline, a toolbar and bottom sheets). It edits the same Candidate, with undo and redo.
- **Asset Panel:** the Advanced Edit's left sidebar on a desktop: the asset tabs to add from — Text, the Stickers tab, Media, Audio, Transitions and the Filters tab. The Workspace widens it or collapses it away.
- **Inspector:** the Advanced Edit's right sidebar on a desktop: the fields of the Pick, one item's or the shared fields of several. The Workspace widens it or collapses it away.
- **Workspace:** how the Advanced Edit's desktop is laid out — the Asset Panel, the player, the Inspector and the Bottom tabs — in the two sidebars' widths, the Bottom tabs' height, and whether the Asset Panel or the Inspector is collapsed (hidden, so the player and Timeline take the width). Dragged to size at its Dividers, never past each panel's least size nor the player's; reset to the default sizes. Remembered by the browser alone; the phone's layout is fixed and keeps its bottom sheets. See docs/adr/0024-the-workspace-is-hand-built-and-browser-remembered.md.
- **Divider:** a boundary of the Workspace dragged to size the panels on either side — one beside the Asset Panel, one beside the Inspector, one above the Bottom tabs. A separator for the keyboard too: the arrows size it, a double-click resets it to its default.
- **Bottom tabs:** the Advanced Edit's tabbed area under the player on a desktop, at one height for them all: the Timeline and the Keyframes pane, with Meters to come. Sized by the Divider above it; never collapsed, only shortened.
- **Main Track:** the Clip's video in the Advanced Edit: its Segments, back to back.
- **Segment:** a piece of the Main Track: a range of the Source, its ends snapped to Words; or part of a video from the Media Library (placed by its Framing, with its own sound's volume, captioned from its Media Words unless its Captions switch is off). It plays its range at its Speed, forwards or in Reverse, or holds one frame as a Freeze frame; its length on the Timeline is its range's divided by its Speed. A Clip that isn't cut is one Segment (`start` to `end`); with several, `start` and `end` are the hull of what they show. Its sound has a volume (under the Clip's own, for the Source), Keyframes and fades, and may be Muted or Detached. Word Edits and Layout Overrides apply in every Segment that shows their time (docs/adr/0002-segments-and-timeline-time.md).
- **Media Library:** the videos, songs and images the owner uploads for the Advanced Edit, kept for every Job, each normalized on upload. An item a Candidate uses can't be deleted.
- **Media Words:** the Words of a video from the Media Library: its whole sound transcribed once, in the item's own language if one is set, else the Job's, and kept on this Mac beside it. Every Segment, Candidate and Job that shows the item reads the same Media Words; corrections to them are Word Edits of each Candidate. A failed transcription is remembered until a Retry, and fails the render of a Clip that shows the item; "Transcribe again" replaces them.
- **Music Clip:** part of a song (or of a video's sound) from the Media Library, or of the Source's sound once Detached, heard under the Clip from a time in Timeline time, at its volume and Speed, forwards or in Reverse, faded in and out, Ducked under speech unless switched off. The Clip's own sound has a volume too.
- **Detach:** moving a Segment's sound off it onto a Music Clip of the same range, Speed, volume and Audio Effects, which then moves on its own; the Segment is Muted. Reattach deletes the Music Clips Detached from that Segment and unmutes it, keeping nothing changed on them.
- **Muted:** a Segment, Music Clip or video Overlay switched silent (M), keeping its volume and Keyframes. Saved and rendered, unlike a muted Track.
- **Audio Effect:** a named treatment of an item's sound from the effects catalogue (such as Reduce noise, Enhance voice, Echo or Telephone), with its own settings. A Segment, a Music Clip and an Overlay video each have an ordered stack of them, applied one after another before the item's volume and fades; each can be switched off without being removed. Constant over its item's length. See docs/adr/0017-audio-effects-are-a-stack-heard-through-effected-copies.md.
- **Ducking:** a Music Clip turned down (by its depth, in dB) while speech is heard: the Words of the Source Segments and Detached sound that aren't Muted, wherever they play on the Timeline. A Music Clip of the Source's sound is never Ducked. See docs/adr/0016-ducking-from-words-and-detached-sound-is-a-music-clip.md.
- **Meters:** the Advanced Edit's peak level of the preview's sound, left and right, beside the Timeline (desktop only), with a light that latches when it clips.
- **Overlay:** a video or image from the Media Library laid over the Clip (picture in picture), in Timeline time: its Transform and Crop, its corners (a radius as a fraction of its shorter side), border and shadow, its in/out animation (the Text Layer's), and a video's sound (muted by default). A video's may have a Speed and play in Reverse. Drawn over the Segments and under the Watermark.
- **Grade:** the colour of a picture: its Adjust, then a Look at an intensity (0–100%). Constant over its item's length, never keyed. A Segment (of the Source or a media item) and an Overlay may each have one; the Candidate's own Grade is laid over the whole Main Track after its Segments' and its Transitions, never over the Overlays. Neutral by default.
- **Adjust:** the dials of a Grade: temperature, tint, saturation, exposure, brightness, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, sharpen, fade and vignette. Not an Adjustment Layer.
- **Look:** a named, ready-made colour from the Filters tab, in a category (Cinematic, Film, Retro, Portrait, Black & White, Vibe, Imported): a set of Adjust values, with or without a colour table of its own. A Grade's Look is mixed over its Adjust by its intensity. Built-in Looks never change. See docs/adr/0013-a-grade-is-folded-curves-and-a-matrix-imitated-in-svg.md.
- **Imported Look:** a Look made from a `.cube` file the owner imports, kept for every Job: neutral Adjust and the file's 3D table. Each import is a new Look, never a replacement; one can't be deleted while anything uses it. The preview only approximates its table.
- **Filters tab:** the Asset Panel's tab of Look cards by category, each showing the frame at the playhead in its Look. Hovering one tries it on the preview; a click gives it to the Pick's Grades (or the Candidate's, with nothing picked); dragging one onto the Timeline makes an Adjustment Layer.
- **Compare render:** the server's render of one frame at the playhead, shown under a split line over the paused preview, to check a Grade's exact pixels. Never kept.
- **Adjustment Layer:** a span of Timeline time on the Adjust Track that lays a Grade over everything under it: the Main Track and the Overlays, not the Watermark, captions, Shapes, Text Layers or hook. It has a strength (0–100%) and fades in and out. Overlapping ones apply one after another.
- **Transition:** how one Segment gives way to the next at their join: one of the Transitions tab's kinds, in seven groups (Basic, Slide, Wipe, Shape, Zoom, Light, Glitch). It may have a strength (for the looks that have one) or a colour (Dip to colour). Its sound is Linear, Equal power or Cut. The two overlap for its length (0.1–3 s, at most half the shorter), so the Clip is that much shorter. See docs/adr/0012-transitions-are-recipes-around-one-xfade.md. Not the Job's status history (`StatusChange`).
- **Text Layer:** a free text overlay added in the Advanced Edit: its words (lines split at newlines and wrapped at its Text Style's wrap width), its time in Timeline time, its Transform (position, scale, rotation and opacity, which Keyframes can move), its Text Style and its in/out animation. Rendered as a picture (docs/adr/0010-text-layers-are-rasterised-pictures.md). Drawn over the captions and the Shapes, and under the hook.
- **Text Style:** how a Text Layer's words look:
  - its face, size and colour;
  - its stroke and drop shadow (colour, opacity, blur, distance, angle);
  - its plate (a Block or Strips, with colour, opacity, radius and padding);
  - its line height, tracking, alignment, wrap width and fixed height (the box's outer size, the plate's padding included).

  The box grows to hold the words; they are never shrunk to fit.
- **Text Preset:** a named Text Style that a new Text Layer starts from. It is copied into the layer, never linked to it. Built-in Presets can't be changed or deleted; the owner saves, renames and deletes their own. One Preset is used for new titles, by the editor and the agent. Not a Style Preset.
- **Shape:** a figure added in the Advanced Edit from the Stickers tab: its time in Timeline time, its Transform (position, scale, rotation and opacity, which Keyframes can move; never stretched nor flipped), its Shape Style and its in/out animation (the Text Layer's). Rendered as a picture, as a Text Layer is (docs/adr/0011-shapes-are-pictures-like-text-layers.md). Drawn over the captions and under the Text Layers.
- **Shape Style:** how a Shape looks: its Shape Kind, its width and height in output pixels (before its Transform's scale), its fill (or none, for an outline alone), its stroke's colour and width (drawn inside its width and height), a rectangle's corner radius (a fraction of its shorter side; 0.5 makes a pill) and its drop shadow (as a Text Layer's). A line and an arrow are all stroke: their width is their length, their stroke width their thickness, their colour the stroke's (a figure with no stroke switched to one takes its fill's colour and a stroke 12 px thick), and their height and fill aren't used (kept for switching back).
- **Shape Kind:** the figure a Shape draws: rectangle, ellipse, triangle (point up), parallelogram (leaning right), trapezoid (narrower at the top), line, or arrow (its head at the right end). Concat's square and circle; with a width and height of its own, a figure needn't have equal sides.
- **Picture layer:** a Text Layer or a Shape, as the planner, the renderer and the preview handle both: an item drawn as the picture libass makes once of its layout, placed by its Transform and Keyframes and animated in and out, between the captions and the hook (docs/adr/0010, docs/adr/0011). Not an Overlay, whose picture is a Media Library item.
- **Stickers tab:** the Advanced Edit's asset tab of figures to add: one shelf, Shapes, a tile per Shape Kind. Image stickers are not part of it.
- **Face:** one font file of a family, at one weight and slant. A style names a Face; weight and italic choose another Face of the same family. Bundled Faces come with the app. Imported Faces are added by the owner for every Job: a variable font is split into a Face per named instance, and a Face can't be deleted while anything uses it. One bundled CJK Face is never named by a style: a Text Layer's characters its own Face hasn't got are set in it.
- **Speed:** the constant rate a Segment, a video Overlay or a Music Clip plays at, 0.0625–16× (1 by default). The pitch is kept unless "Change pitch with speed" is on. Words of a Source Segment are retimed with it. Not the preview rate, a viewing aid. See docs/adr/0009-speed-keeps-the-range-and-reverse-is-a-derived-copy.md.
- **Reverse:** a Segment, a video Overlay or a Music Clip playing its range backwards, from a Retimed copy. A reversed Segment of the Source has no captions.
- **Retimed copy:** a file derived from a range of the Source or a media item, reversed (or, for the preview, retimed at a Speed outside 0.25–4×), kept on this Mac beside its Job or item. Any copy of a wider range of the same item serves.
- **Effected copy:** an item's sound as the render hears it before its volume and fades (its range, Speed, Reverse and Audio Effects), made by the server for the preview and kept on this Mac until its Audio Effects change. Until it is ready, the preview plays the item's sound without them.
- **Freeze frame:** a Segment that holds one frame of the Source or a video for its hold, silent, framed as its Segment was at that moment. Made by splitting a Segment at the playhead (Shift+F); it ripples the Main Track. The ends of the Segments beside it that touch its frame stay there, not snapped to Words.
- **Pick:** the items picked on the Advanced Edit's Timeline or preview, which its actions and inspector act on: one or more Segments, Text Layers, Shapes, Music Clips and Overlays, mixed; or the hook, the captions, a Cue or a join, alone. Not Selection (the AI's) nor a Candidate selected for rendering. Kept in the editor only, never saved or undone; items removed by an undo or another editor drop out of it.
- **Clipboard:** the Advanced Edit's copied items, kept in memory while the browser tab is open, keeping their order and their times relative to one another. They paste into any Candidate, of this Job or another; Segments of a Source only into its own Job, bringing the Word Edits, Cue Edits and Layout Overrides of their range where the Candidate has none; Segments of a media item into any Candidate, bringing its Word Edits and Cue Edits likewise.
- **Track:** a row of the Advanced Edit's Timeline by kind: Text, Shape, Adjust, Overlay, Video (the Main Track), Captions, Hook and Music. Text Layers, Shapes, Adjustment Layers, Overlays and Music Clips are spread over as many lanes as their overlaps need, worked out each time, never saved. A Track can be hidden or muted in the preview and locked against hand edits (Text, Shape, Adjust, Overlay, Video, Captions and Hook hide; Overlay, Video and Music mute; every Track locks). These are kept in the editor only: never saved, never undone, and the Clip renders as if none were set.
- **Magnetic timeline:** the Advanced Edit's switch (on by default, remembered by the browser) under which Text Layers, Shapes, Adjustment Layers, Music Clips, Overlays and the hook follow the Main Track when it gets shorter or longer at a point: deleting, trimming or inserting Segments. Those after the point move with it; those across it lose what was cut; those wholly inside a cut go, except the hook, which moves to the join. Reordering Segments and Transitions move nothing. The clip editor's trim follows the same switch. See docs/adr/0004-layers-ripple-with-the-main-track.md.
- **Ripple delete:** Shift+Delete: a delete that closes the gap whatever the Magnetic timeline's switch says. On a Segment, the layers follow; on a Text Layer, Shape, Adjustment Layer, Music Clip or Overlay, the later items of its Track move up by its length.
- **Split all:** splitting at the playhead every Segment, Text Layer, Shape, Adjustment Layer, Music Clip and Overlay under it, except on locked Tracks (Cmd/Ctrl+B with nothing picked). The hook is never split; the captions break their Cues at the new join, as at every join.
- **Filmstrip:** the frames of a Segment's video drawn along it on the Timeline, one for each whole second of the Source or the media item; each tile shows the frame under its middle. The frames are the video as it is, not framed by the Layout.
- **Peaks:** the highest and lowest sample of each millisecond of a sound (the Source's, or a media item's), kept on this Mac to draw Waveforms.
- **Waveform:** the sound of a Music Clip or a Segment drawn along it on the Timeline from its Peaks, shaped by its volume and fades.
- **Volume Line:** a line across a Music Clip or a Segment on the Timeline at its volume (100% at two thirds of its height), following its volume Keyframes where it has them. Dragging it on a picked item sets the volume, as one step of undo. A Source Segment's is its own volume, under the Clip's own. Where a Music Clip is Ducked, the stretch under the line is shaded.
- **Fade Ramp:** a Music Clip's or a Segment's fade in or out, drawn as a slope at its start or end. On a picked one, its handle drags the fade's length.
- **Zoom to fit:** zooming the Timeline so that the Pick fills it, or, with nothing picked, the whole Clip (1×). Never past the Timeline's deepest zoom.
- **Keyframe:** the value of one property of an item at one time, in seconds from the item's start (from its Scene's start on the Source's timeline, for a Manual Layout Override). An Overlay's, a Text Layer's, a Shape's, a media Segment's or a Manual Scene's position, scale, rotation and opacity, and the volume of a Music Clip, an Overlay video or a Segment, can have them. While a property has Keyframes they replace its constant; between two, the value moves by the later one's Ease. Trimming or splitting an item keeps its motion on the same moments; changing its Speed scales them, and Reverse mirrors them, keeping its motion on the same frames. See docs/adr/0008-keyframes-for-pictures-and-sound.md.
- **Ease:** how a Keyframe is approached from the one before it: a cubic-bezier curve, Linear, Ease in, Ease out, Ease in-out, or Custom.
- **Auto-key:** once a property of an item has Keyframes, every change to it in the Advanced Edit (a field, the Grips, the Volume Line, Fit or Fill) writes a Keyframe at the playhead instead of changing its constant.
- **Keyframes pane:** the Advanced Edit's second bottom tab, beside the Timeline (desktop only): the picked item's keyable properties as rows, shown as a Dopesheet or as Curves.
- **Dopesheet:** the Keyframes pane's view of a lane per property with its Keyframes as diamonds, to retime them.
- **Curves:** the Keyframes pane's view of each keyed property's value drawn over time, with a dot per Keyframe and two handles per span that shape its Ease.
- **Edit Point:** a place on the Advanced Edit's Timeline where something starts or ends: a join of Segments, an edge of a Text Layer, Shape, Adjustment Layer, Music Clip or Overlay, an edge of the hook, or either end of the Clip. ↑ and ↓ move the playhead to the one before or after it, skipping hidden Tracks.
- **Seam:** a Transition as the Advanced Edit's Timeline draws it: a band over the two Segments' overlap, with a grip at each edge. Dragging either grip lengthens or shortens it from both edges at once, within its bounds, as one step of undo. A join with no Transition is a diamond.
- **Skim:** the preview showing the frame under the pointer while it moves over the Advanced Edit's Timeline, the playhead staying where it is; with the pointer gone, the preview is back at the playhead. Switched with S and remembered by the browser; the Skim's sound is a second switch, off by default. Only while paused, and not on a phone.
- **Grips:** the handles on a picked picture on the preview (an Overlay, a Text Layer, a Shape, a media Segment, or the Source in a Manual Scene): corners scale it about its centre, edges stretch it, the handle above it rotates it. The captions, or a picked Cue on its own, are moved and scaled the same way, never stretched nor rotated. A Text Layer's are on its words' box, and it has no edges to stretch. A Shape's edges change its Shape Style's width or height instead, so its stroke and corners keep their size (a line's and an arrow's: only the ends, their length). One drag is one step of undo.
- **Smart Guide:** a line shown while a picture is dragged, where its edge or centre meets an edge or centre of the frame, of another picture shown at the playhead, or a fixed guide; it snaps there (Alt: no snapping).
- **Crop mode:** the preview showing a picked picture whole and dimmed, with handles on its edges and corners that move its Crop. Enter or a click outside keeps it; Escape drops it.
- **Loop Region:** the stretch between the In and Out marks (set at the playhead with I and O) that playback repeats while Loop is on (L). Kept in the editor only: never saved, never undone.
- **Timeline time:** seconds into the output Clip. What the Advanced Edit adds, and the hook, are anchored to it; Word Edits, Cue Edits and Layout Overrides stay on the Source's timeline (a media item's Word Edits and Cue Edits, on its own).
